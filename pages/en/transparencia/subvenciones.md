# <span aria-hidden="true">💶</span> Grants

<EnConstruccion motivo="the National Grants Database has a public API, but it returns millions of paginated awards from every administration; they have to be downloaded in batches, each awarding body classified by administration and the categories cleaned before any figures can be published." />

## What it will show

The General Grants Law sets **competitive tendering** as the rule: a public call and
allocation according to criteria. The exceptions are **nominative** grants (named in the
budget) and **direct award** grants on grounds of public interest. This section will measure:

- What share of grant money is awarded without competition, by administration and by year.
- The main awarding bodies and beneficiaries (legal entities; never data on natural
  persons).
- Its trend in real euros per inhabitant and the comparison **by party** of the government awarding them.

## Where the data will come from

- [National Grants Database (BDNS) / National System for the Publication of Grants and Public
  Aid](https://www.infosubvenciones.es/bdnstrans/GE/es/index), with calls and awards from
  all administrations since around 2016, accessible through its public API.

In the meantime, the [list of open data projects](/en/varios/datos-abiertos) includes this source.
