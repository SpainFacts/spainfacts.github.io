# <span aria-hidden="true">📨</span> Freedom of information complaints

<EnConstruccion motivo="the Council of Transparency and Good Governance publishes its rulings only as monthly web listings with one PDF per ruling; the outcome can be inferred from the PDF path, but the ministry or body concerned only appears inside the document, so thousands of PDFs have to be read and classified." />

## What it will show

Law 19/2013 recognises everyone's right to request public information. When an
administration refuses it or does not reply, a complaint can be filed with the Council of Transparency and Good Governance
(or with the equivalent regional body). This section will measure:

- How many complaints are resolved each year and with what outcome: **upheld** (the Council rules in the
  citizen's favour), partially upheld, dismissed or inadmissible.
- Which ministries and bodies accumulate the most upheld complaints, in proportion to their size.
- Non-compliance with the rulings and appeals by the Administration against them.
- The comparison **by government and party**, using the same criteria as the rest of the section.

## Where the data will come from

- [Rulings of the Council of Transparency and Good Governance](https://www.consejodetransparencia.es/),
  national scope, since 2015.
- The Council's annual reports and Transparency Portal statistics on access requests
  (currently only in PDF).

In the meantime, the [list of open data projects](/en/varios/datos-abiertos) includes these sources.
